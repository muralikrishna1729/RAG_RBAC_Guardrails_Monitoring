## How to Apply

1. Email your updated resume to **careers@company.com** with the position
   title in the subject line, or
2. Apply through the internal HRMS portal under *Careers > Open Requisitions*.

- Internal candidates receive priority review and should inform their current
  reporting manager before applying.
- Typical hiring timeline: profile screening (3-5 days), technical/functional
  interview round(s), HR discussion, offer roll-out within 2 weeks.

## Referral Program

Employees who refer a candidate that completes 90 days of employment receive a
referral bonus of INR 25,000 (manager and above roles: INR 40,000), paid in the
next payroll cycle.

## Benefits Summary

- Health insurance (employee + dependents), term life cover
- 24 paid leaves per year, plus 12 public/regional holidays
- Learning & development stipend of INR 30,000 per year
- Flexible working hours and hybrid schedule
